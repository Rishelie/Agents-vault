# Vault protocol

Work starts with **SCOPE** from [[catalog]].

- Read/write **only** that folder (`vault/projects/<slug>/` or `vault/ops/` or a listed universe path).
- Do not load Kassandra `MEMORY.md` into other agents.
- After each **finished** job: append `log/YYYY-MM-DD.md` and dated facts in that folder’s `MEMORY.md`.
- Link slugs with [[wiki-links]] (`[[nexus]]`, `[[studio]]`).
- Heartbeat off. Do not wake idle specialists.
- **APPROVE MAP** required before migrating old `vault/production|real-estate|education` notes into projects.
