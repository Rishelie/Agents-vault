# NJ Collision Shop Automation

This page records the reviewed Ziggy workflow and the current setup-and-leave offer. Pricing and outreach order are time-sensitive and must be refreshed after 2026-10-17.

## Confirmed customer flow

A public mobile form collects the customer's name, contact details, vehicle or damage details, and photos. Customers do not log into the private shop desk.

## Confirmed shop flow

The shop keeps one card per repair with photos, contact details, notes, and staff-controlled stages:

`New → Estimate → Parts → Paint → Ready → Completed`

Paint means the vehicle is in the paint booth. The workflow does not estimate insurance work or communicate with insurers.

## Current offer

As of 2026-09-17:

- $2,500 for the ticket board and agent workflow on a client-owned host.
- Optional $750 one-page call site.
- The client owns and pays for hosting and provider accounts.
- Fourteen days of break/fix support are included; later support is a separate engagement.
- Missed-call handling, SMS, automatic pricing, and long-term custody of client credentials are outside the base offer.

## Outreach boundary

Prospect gaps remain hypotheses until confirmed. Draft emails require explicit approval before sending. The current target order is Ziggy, Centro, then Brookdale, subject to ownership and contact verification.
